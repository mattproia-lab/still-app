Common questions

How do subscriptions and the free trial work?

Still offers an optional subscription for full access, including a 7-day free trial. Reading is always free. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Settings under your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

What are audio credits?

Hearing the voices spoken aloud uses optional audio credit. Reading is always included at no cost. Audio credit is a one-time purchase that draws down only when you choose to listen.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you've reinstalled the app or switched devices, use the "Restore Purchases" option in the app to recover an active subscription. If anything doesn't restore correctly, email us and we'll make it right.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing admin@stillprayer.app. See our Privacy Policy for details on how we handle your information.

Something isn't working

If you run into a problem, email us at admin@stillprayer.app with a short description of what happened and, if you can, the device you're using. It helps us fix things quickly.
