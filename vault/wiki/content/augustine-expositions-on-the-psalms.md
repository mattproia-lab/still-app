## Three things to know before using it for the Office

1. **It is an abridgement.** Coxe says so on the title page and in the preface: the general editor allowed one volume for the six of the Oxford translation, "one hundred pages to each", and he "preserved the African Psalter entire, with as much of the comment as was possible" ([raw lines 88–100](../../raw/theology/doctors/2026-09-08-augustine-expositions-on-the-psalms-npnf1-08.md)). Ellipses in the text ("1. …When ye hear sung in the Psalms", Psalm CXIII) mark his cuts. A full English of the Enarrationes is the six Oxford volumes themselves, on archive.org as scans, not filed.
2. **The psalm headings use the Hebrew (English Bible) numbering**, not the Vulgate's that the app's Douay text and the Lectionary's `dr` block use: "Psalm CXXX" is "Out of the deep", "Psalm CXIX" the alphabetic psalm, "Psalm X" "Why, O Lord, hast Thou withdrawn afar off" ([raw lines 62168, 56724 and 4163](../../raw/theology/doctors/2026-09-08-augustine-expositions-on-the-psalms-npnf1-08.md)). To set a commentary beside a Vulgate-numbered psalm, apply the same table as the Lectionary page: Vulgate 9 is Hebrew 9–10, Vulgate 10–112 are Hebrew 11–113, Vulgate 113 is Hebrew 114–115, Vulgate 114–115 are Hebrew 116, Vulgate 116–145 are Hebrew 117–146, Vulgate 146–147 are Hebrew 147 ([lectionary.md](lectionary.md)). The verses quoted inside each exposition are Augustine's own psalter, "the African Psalter" Coxe kept entire ([raw line 100](../../raw/theology/doctors/2026-09-08-augustine-expositions-on-the-psalms-npnf1-08.md)), rendered by the Oxford translators; they will not match the Douay word for word.
3. **One heading is mislabelled in the transcription.** The exposition of Psalm XIII ("How long, O Lord, wilt Thou forget me unto the end?") is headed "Psalm XII." a second time at [raw line 5036](../../raw/theology/doctors/2026-09-08-augustine-expositions-on-the-psalms-npnf1-08.md), after the true Psalm XII at [raw line 4896](../../raw/theology/doctors/2026-09-08-augustine-expositions-on-the-psalms-npnf1-08.md). The raw file is left as found; anything that indexes the file by heading must treat the second "Psalm XII." as XIII.
