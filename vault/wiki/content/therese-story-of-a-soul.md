## What the etext leaves out or changes, by its own account

The transcriber's note ([raw lines 33–75](../../raw/theology/doctors/2026-09-08-therese-of-lisieux-story-of-a-soul-taylor.md)) is explicit, and a reader should know it before quoting:

- Not included: the illustrations and their list, the accounts of favours attributed to her intercession, the documents of the beatification, the letters of Pius X and others, and "some other material not written by her"; the contents list marks the omitted sections "[omitted]" ([raw lines 159–165](../../raw/theology/doctors/2026-09-08-therese-of-lisieux-story-of-a-soul-taylor.md)).
- Footnotes were renumbered by chapter and a few modified or dropped; the psalm citations carry the modern number in brackets after the Vulgate number, "Psalm 22[23]:1-4"; a few typographical errors were corrected in brackets; the original page headers and the printed disclaimer about calling her "saint" before canonisation were left out.
- So this is Taylor's translation of everything Thérèse wrote that the 1912 book carried, but not the whole 1912 book. The full book, with the favours and the beatification documents, would need the scan.
