## Edition

- Title page (both volumes): "The Paradise or Garden of the Holy Fathers, being histories of the anchorites, recluses, monks, coenobites and ascetic fathers of the deserts of Egypt between A.D. CCL and A.D. CCCC circiter, compiled by Athanasius Archbishop of Alexandria, Palladius Bishop of Helenopolis, Saint Jerome and others; now translated out of the Syriac with notes and introduction by Ernest A. Wallis Budge, M.A., Litt.D., D.Lit., Keeper of the Assyrian and Egyptian Antiquities in the British Museum; published at London by Chatto & Windus, MCMVII" ([vol. 1, raw lines 76–105](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-1.md); [vol. 2, raw lines 80–109](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-2.md)). Budge's preface is dated "British Museum, June 5, 1907" ([vol. 1, raw line 181](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-1.md)).
- Volume I "containing the Introduction; the Life of St Anthony by Athanasius; Histories of the Fathers by Palladius; the Rule of Pachomius; St Jerome's History of the Fathers"; Volume II "containing the Counsels of the Holy Men and the Questions and Answers of the Ascetic Brethren, generally known as the Sayings of the Fathers of Egypt" ([vol. 1, raw lines 28–37](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-1.md); [vol. 2, raw lines 28–46](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-2.md)).
- The text behind it: the Syriac "Book of Paradise" in the seventh-century redaction of Anan-Isho, from a manuscript copied at Mosul in 1888, first published with the Syriac for private circulation in 1904; this 1907 edition is the translation alone, for the public ([Budge's preface, vol. 1, raw lines 117–161](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-1.md)).
- Public domain in the United States: published 1907, before 1929, as the title page's MCMVII states.
- Source scans: the PIMS (University of Toronto) copies, digitized 2011 — <https://archive.org/details/paradiseorgarden01ann> and <https://archive.org/details/paradiseorgarden02ann>, plain text at the `_djvu.txt` of each, fetched 2026-09-08. Four other scans of the same edition exist on archive.org (Princeton, Harvard, Michigan, California copies); their OCR is of the same quality within a few hundred words, and a matched pair from one library was taken. Project Gutenberg has no record of the work (searched 2026-09-08).

## Caveats

- **OCR, not a clean text.** The archive.org text separates words with two spaces, keeps running heads and page numbers, and mangles the decorative headings and running titles (the Life of Anthony's running head reads "Ubc %itc ot Saint Bntbon\?"); the section headings themselves are mostly unreadable in the OCR, which is why the pointers above go to the first sentence of each section rather than its heading. The Syriac frontispiece captions and the library stamps are noise. Anyone quoting from it should read the passage in the page scan at the source URL before use.
- The digitization notice ("Digitized by the Internet Archive in 2011 with funding from University of Toronto") stands at the head of each file as the scan carries it ([vol. 1, raw lines 40–43](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-1.md)).
- Budge's translation is from the Syriac, not from the Greek of Athanasius and Palladius; the preface and introduction explain the relation (Dom Cuthbert Butler's Greek Lausiac History is cited there, [vol. 1, raw lines 147–151](../../raw/theology/saints/2026-09-08-budge-paradise-of-the-holy-fathers-vol-1.md)).
