## Caveats

- **OCR, not a clean text.** Running heads ("Ch. 12] THE DEVOUT LIFE", "INTRODUCTION TO [Pt. 3"), page numbers, hyphenated line breaks and scanner noise on the blank pages remain; a few heading lines are garbled. Anyone quoting from it should read the passage in the page scan at the source URL before use.
- **Chapter headings were not machine-countable** (the OCR breaks some); presence of the chapters was checked by their titles instead, see below.

## Why this edition, and the others seen

- CCEL's text (<https://ccel.org/ccel/desales/devout_life>) is clean but comes from the Rivingtons "Library of Spiritual Works for English Catholics" (1876), an edition for the Church of England: its footnote 4 says "The address to Philothea by name has been omitted … and the term child or daughter used instead", and the 1891 Longmans printing of the same series says in its editor's preface that "Two whole chapters have been omitted, one for doctrinal reasons" (<https://archive.org/details/IntroductionToTheDevoutLife>, OCR lines 391–420). Not filed.
- Methuen, 1906, translated with notes by the Rev. Thomas Barns, M.A. (Oxon.), from the 1619 text, "no attempt … either to abridge it or to adapt it" (<https://archive.org/details/introductiontode00fran_1>): complete, with a long apparatus in Latin, French and Italian; an alternative if a clearly pre-1929 *scan* is wanted.
- F. Pustet, Ratisbon and New York, about 1900, "Introduction to a Devout Life, from the French" (<https://archive.org/details/introductiontodefran>): the older anonymous English translation ("as Glycera did her nosegays"), Catholic, no translator named.
- Project Gutenberg returns no record for the title (searched 2026-09-08).
