Saint of the Day
May 3, 2026 · Feast Day
St. Philip
the Apostle

d. c. 80 AD · Apostle to Greece and Phrygia

Philip was the apostle who asked: "Lord, show us the Father, and it is sufficient for us." It was not an arrogant demand. It was the truest prayer a human being can pray — the exhaustion of trying to understand God through concepts, and the desire to simply see. Christ's answer was not a vision. It was a face: "He who has seen Me has seen the Father." Philip wanted a theophany. He got an incarnation. He was looking for God in the spectacular, and God was standing three feet away asking him to look.

"Lord, show us the Father, and it is sufficient for us."

A prayer from his tradition

Holy Philip, apostle and witness — you spent three years asking the questions we still ask. Help us to look again at what is already in front of us, and to see.
